Who we are

Live Jury (shown in the app as "The Jury Room") is a social deliberation game published independently by Vedika Yog ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it.

The short version: we collect only what the game needs, we never sell it or use it for ads, and you can delete everything at any time.

What we collect

If you create an account, we store:

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. We never see them.
  • Profile: your display name, points and in-app preferences (haptics, notifications).
  • Game activity: rooms you join, exhibits you review, your ballots and written reasons, chat messages you send in a jury room, and your case journal.
  • Safety records: messages you report, and reports made about your messages.
  • In-app notices: notifications shown inside the app and whether you have read them.

Demo mode needs no account. Demo progress is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The app and this website have no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your profile and progress across devices.
  • To run jury rooms: seating you with other jurors, showing your display name and messages to people in the same room, and calculating results and points.
  • To show the leaderboard, which lists display names and points.
  • To keep conversations respectful, by reviewing reported messages and suspending accounts that break the rules.
  • To send password-reset emails when you ask for one.

Your individual ballots are visible only to you. Other jurors see only the room's combined result. We do not sell or rent your data, or share it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles authentication and email. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Google Firebase Hosting serves the web version of the app.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website.

Like any web host, Firebase Hosting and GitHub Pages may briefly log technical request data such as IP addresses to keep their services secure. We don't receive or use those logs.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, profile, ballots, messages, case journal, notices and reports are permanently erased right away.

Rooms you took part in stay in other jurors' journals, but without your name, your messages or your ballots.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time:

  • In the app: open You (your profile), then tap Delete account.
  • On this website: use the account deletion form and sign in to confirm.
  • By email: write to vedika.yog@gmail.com from your account's email address. We will delete the account within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

Live Jury is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
