What we do

A game about thinking it through, together.

Live Jury turns a small mystery into a shared conversation. There is no single right answer to memorise. The points go to careful reading, good evidence and clear reasons.

Original case files

Each case is written and illustrated from scratch, with its own parties, timeline and question to resolve. Nothing is based on real people.

An evidence desk

Study documents, schematics and witness transcripts. Each exhibit shows where it came from and how it could be read either way.

A private first impression

Cast a sealed first vote before anyone speaks, so the room hears six honest starting points instead of an echo.

Real deliberation

Discuss with five other jurors, cite exhibits right in the chat, and change your mind when the evidence earns it.

An anonymous verdict

Final ballots stay private. The room only sees the combined result, and split or hung juries are valid outcomes.

Rewards for thinking

Earn points and badges for reviewing every exhibit, citing evidence and explaining your reasoning, never for agreeing with the crowd.

How it works

One case, four moments.

A round takes about the length of a coffee. Play with friends using a private room code, or join an open jury.

  1. 1

    Take your seat

    Join an open room or share a six-letter code with friends.

  2. 2

    Examine

    Read the case and review every exhibit before your first vote.

  3. 3

    Deliberate

    Talk it through, cite evidence and hear the other five.

  4. 4

    Decide

    Cast a sealed final ballot with a reason, then see the verdict.
