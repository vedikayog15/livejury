Account deletion

Delete your Jury Room account

The Jury Room is published by Vedika Yog. Sign in below to permanently delete your account. You can also do this in the app: open You, then tap Delete account.

What is deleted, right away and permanently:
  • Your account, email address and password
  • Your display name, points and preferences
  • Your ballots, written reasons and chat messages
  • Your case journal, notices and reports
What stays: rooms you took part in stay in other jurors' journals, but without your name, messages or ballots. We keep nothing else.

Can't sign in or forgot your password? Reset it from the app's sign-in screen, or email vedika.yog@gmail.com from your account's email address. We will delete your account within 30 days.
